## Restoration bug found by the real fixture

The first strict comparison found missing wheat at lab `(7,66,8)` inside the cactus selection. A small native regression reproduced it with mature wheat above farmland: the saved palette contained `minecraft:wheat[age=7]`, while native placement returned air despite final farmland support and light 15. Native placement checked the plant before the complete surrounding template had been restored. Simply reapplying the crop afterward also left dropped copies and failed the entity-safety assertion.

`PaperStasisWorld` now defers growth blocks during native placement using the public `BlockTransformer`, then restores saved plain block data without physics once support geometry and native tile NBT are complete. No snapshot-format change is needed. The regression checks immediate exact restoration, absence of extra dropped entities through the existing entity checks, disk recovery/failed-restore retry, and mature wheat surviving subsequent ticks. The full native snapshot suite, including entities, 52 signs, 41 extended materials and four moving-piston cases, passes. The exact imported-world comparison also passes. Diagnostic logging was removed from production code.

This is a later production fix than the [earlier #11 report](results.md), whose recorded JAR hash and results remain historical.
